# Design references used for this pass

These are references for design quality and interaction thinking, not templates to copy.

- Dennis Snellenberg — https://dennissnellenberg.com/
  - restrained typography, whitespace, direct freelance positioning
- Stefan Vitasovic 2025 — https://stefanvitasovic.dev/
  - offset Swiss-inspired layout, typographic motion, repeating motion motifs
- Arnaud Rocca — https://arnaudrocca.fr/
  - minimalist creative-developer portfolio with strong GSAP motion system
- Thibault Guignand — https://www.thibaultguignand.com/
  - GSAP + Lenis synced on one ticker; tightly controlled grid and motion
- Stas Bondar — https://stabondar.com/
  - motion-driven portfolio where interaction itself communicates technical craft
- Joffrey Spitzer — Codrops 2026 case study
  - restrained minimal design, subtle transitions, micro-detail over visual clutter
- Lusion — https://lusion.co/
  - large typographic moments, confident spacing, strong motion rhythm

Rules taken from the references:
1. Motion must have a repeated language; don't randomly animate every component.
2. Large type + empty space should do more work than cards and decorations.
3. Scroll and scroll-linked animation must run from one consistent timing source.
4. Use asymmetric/offset details sparingly to stop the layout feeling templated.
5. Keep interaction noticeable but never make the site harder to read or navigate.
